Roster ({{ entries|length }} entries)

Upload the pack roster export (CSV or tab-separated). Only Scout First Name, Scout Last Name, Member ID, Den and Status are used; parent contacts, dues and other columns are ignored and not stored. You see a preview before anything changes. Re-importing a newer export updates the roster: scouts are matched by Member ID, so den moves and name fixes update them, and scouts no longer Active are marked inactive. Car numbers are assigned automatically. Scouts are shared across events, so importing the same roster into the regatta reuses them.
